## What is GOHEALTH?

GOHEALTH stands for the **Gombe State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency**. It is the government agency responsible for implementing the Contributory Healthcare Scheme in Gombe State, Nigeria. GOHEALTH's mission is to provide accessible and affordable healthcare to all citizens of Gombe State regardless of their income level or employment status.

## Mandate & Vision

- **Vision:** Universal health coverage for every resident of Gombe State.
- **Mission:** To provide affordable, accessible, and quality healthcare through an efficient contributory health insurance scheme.
- **Established under:** Gombe State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency Law.
