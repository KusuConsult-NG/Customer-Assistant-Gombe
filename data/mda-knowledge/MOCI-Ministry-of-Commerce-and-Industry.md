# MOCI — Ministry of Commerce and Industry
Also known as: Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industries, Ministry of Commerce and Energy

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- Predecessor leadership records maintained under historical state gazettes and executive transition archives.

Over the years has managed state commercial enterprise development, industrial parks, and mineral/energy liaisons.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Facilitating industrial development, promoting domestic and international trade, encouraging small and medium enterprises, organizing international trade fairs, and supervising state cooperative societies.

**Vision**: To position Gombe State as Northern Nigeria’s premier commercial, manufacturing, and agro-allied processing hub.

**Mission**: To stimulate private sector enterprise, industrial growth, and commercial investments through an enabling business climate.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Commerce & Trade Promotion
- Department of Industrial Development
- Department of Cooperative Societies & Associations
- Department of Small & Medium Enterprises Development
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics (PRS)

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Business premises registration and statutory certification
- Registration, supervision, and annual auditing of cooperative societies
- Trade fair and agricultural exhibition participation coordination
- Allocation of commercial stalls in state-managed market plazas
- Export readiness support for local agricultural and artisanal producers

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
- Gombe State Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency (GSMEDA)
- Gombe Investment and Property Development Company (GIPC)
- Gombe Minerals Development Company (GMDC)
