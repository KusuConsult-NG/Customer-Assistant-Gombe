# Gombe State Budget and Financial Intelligence

Also known as: Gombe State Budget, State Appropriation, Gombe Finances, MoBEP Budget Intelligence
Alternative names: Gombe State Annual Budget, Citizens' Budget, Gombe State Appropriation Law, Gombe Revenue and Expenditure

## No budget figures are confirmed

**This service holds no Gombe State budget figures. Do not state a budget size,
an allocation, a percentage, a growth rate or a sectoral share to a caller.**

If a caller asks what the budget is, what their ministry or their LGA was
allocated, or how much was spent on something, say plainly that we do not have
the budget figures and offer to put them through to the **Ministry of Budget and
Economic Planning**, or point them to the State's published Citizens' Budget.

Every figure that stood on this page — the total budget, the capital and
recurrent split, the year-on-year growth, the sectoral allocations — was taken
from another State's appropriation law, and was presented here as Gombe's own,
down to the kobo. Budget figures are the easiest thing in this knowledge base
for a journalist, a councillor or a contractor to check, and the hardest for a
citizen to question in the moment. They were deleted rather than adjusted.

## What is confirmed

- The budget is coordinated, prepared and monitored by the **Ministry of Budget
  and Economic Planning**, and assented to by the **Executive Governor, Sen.
  Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya**, after passage by the **Gombe State House of
  Assembly**.
- **Commissioner for Finance** and **Commissioner for Budget and Economic
  Planning**: NOT CONFIRMED. Gombe swore in 23 Commissioners in July 2026, but
  no source read states which of them holds which portfolio.

## How to read a budget figure, once there are some

These cautions apply to any figure added here, and the assistant should repeat
them when it quotes one:

1. **An approved allocation is a spending ceiling, not money spent.** It does
   not establish that funds were released, or that anything was built.
2. **Capital spending** funds physical infrastructure and equipment;
   **recurrent spending** funds salaries, pensions and overheads.
3. Quote the figure only as the source states it, and name the source and the
   year.

## To whoever fills this in

Take the figures only from: the **Gombe State Appropriation Law** for the year,
the State's **Citizens' Budget**, State Executive Council gazettes, and
published budget implementation reports. Give the year and the source for every
figure. Do not carry a figure over from a previous year's page.
