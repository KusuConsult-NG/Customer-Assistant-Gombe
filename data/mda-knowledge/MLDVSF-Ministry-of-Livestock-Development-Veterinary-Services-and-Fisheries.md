# MLDVSF — Ministry of Livestock Development, Veterinary Services, and Fisheries
Also known as: Ministry of Livestock Development, Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries

- Predecessor leadership records maintained under historical state gazettes and executive transition archives.

Created by Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya in February 2025 by carving out livestock, veterinary clinics, disease surveillance, and aquaculture out of the Ministry of Agriculture. Aligns with the national creation of the Federal Ministry of Livestock Development.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Promote commercial livestock production, pastoralist transformation, ranching and fodder development; control animal diseases; inspect meat and abattoir operations; and boost commercial aquaculture and fisheries.

**Vision**: A modern, disease-free, and commercially prosperous livestock and aquaculture economy fostering peaceful coexistence and high-protein nutrition.

**Mission**: To eliminate transboundary animal diseases, modernize pastoral and fish farming value-chains, and build modern abattoirs and cold-chains across Gombe State.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Livestock Production & Pastoral Development
- Department of Veterinary Clinics & Hospital Services
- Department of Animal Disease Surveillance & Epizootiology
- Department of Fisheries & Aquaculture Development
- Department of Abattoir Management & Meat Inspection

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Veterinary clinical consultations, disease diagnoses, and surgery at state veterinary hospitals
- Annual vaccination campaigns for cattle, sheep, goats, poultry, and dogs (Rabies, CBPP, PPR, Newcastle disease)
- Abattoir ante-mortem and post-mortem meat inspection and public health slaughterhouse oversight
- Commercial catfish and tilapia fingerlings supply and fish feed production guidance
- Pastoralist dispute resolution, grazing reserve demarcation, and fodder bank access

## Key State Facilities
- Gombe Modern Abattoir Complex, Dogon Dutse, Gombe
- Veterinary hospital and clinics across the 20 LGAs
- Dadin Kowa Dam wetlands (Akko LGA)
