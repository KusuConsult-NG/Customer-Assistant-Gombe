# Gombe State House of Assembly & Government Leadership Directory

## Gombe State House of Assembly (Legislative Arm)
- **Current Speaker**: Rt. Hon. Na'anlong Daniel (representing Yamaltu/Deba State Constituency) (as of April 2026).
  - Unanimously elected as Speaker of the 10th Gombe State House of Assembly on **July 2, 2025**.
  - Succeeded former Speaker **Hon. Gabriel Dewan** (representing Young Progressives Party, YPP, for Akko North / Gombe Central), who served from November 2023 until his resignation on July 2, 2025 to balance political stability and power-sharing across geopolitical zones.
- **Deputy Speaker**: Not confirmed in our records (reports from 2025 name different people). Please confirm with the House of Assembly.
- **Clerk of the House**: Managed under the administrative structure of the Assembly Service Commission.
- **Location**: Gombe State House of Assembly Complex, John Mark Samchi Road, Gombe, Gombe State, Nigeria.
- **Mandate**: Lawmaking, legislative oversight over executive MDAs, budget appropriation and vetting, confirmation of commissioner nominees, and representation of Gombe citizens across all 24 state constituencies.
