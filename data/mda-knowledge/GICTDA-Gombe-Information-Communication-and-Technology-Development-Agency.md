# GICTDA — Gombe Information Communication and Technology Development Agency
Also known as: Gombe Information Communication and Technology Development Agency (GICTDA), Gombe State Information and Communication Technology Development Agency, GICTDA

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- Predecessor leadership records maintained under historical state gazettes and executive transition archives.

Established in 2018 to pioneer state digital governance, manage government cloud services, build the Code Gombe tech workforce, and establish IT standards.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate and execute state ICT policy; deploy and manage government email, web portals, and connectivity networks; administer the Code Gombe software engineering training academy; protect government cyber infrastructure; and catalyze the digital economy.

**Vision**: To position Gombe State as Nigeria's leading technological innovation ecosystem and preferred digital outsourcing destination.

**Mission**: To leverage digital technology for open governance, build a globally competitive technical workforce, and automate government services for citizen convenience.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of E-Governance & Software Systems
- Department of Infrastructure, Networks & Cybersecurity
- Department of Digital Skills & Workforce Development (Code Gombe)
- Department of IT Standards, Policy & Regulatory Compliance
- Department of Innovation, Research & Startup Incubation

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Code Gombe Fellowship: Intensive training in full-stack software development, UI/UX design, data science, and cloud computing for youth
- Government official email and subdomain provisioning for MDAs (gombestate.gov.ng)
- Tech startup incubation, co-working facilities, and digital investor linkages
- Open Government Data portal maintenance and ICT equipment standards testing
