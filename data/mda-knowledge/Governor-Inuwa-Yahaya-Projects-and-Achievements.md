# Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya — Projects and Achievements (Gombe State)
Also known as: Governor Inuwa Yahaya Projects, Gombe State Current Projects, What has Governor Inuwa Yahaya done, Gombe State Infrastructure and Projects Scorecard, Roads and Bridges under Inuwa Yahaya.

## No projects scorecard is confirmed

This scorecard has not been compiled for Gombe State. There is no list of roads,
bridges, buses, hospitals, schools or water projects here, and no project,
figure, date or location is confirmed.

If a caller asks what the Government has done, or what is being built in their
Local Government Area, say plainly that we do not have the projects list, and
offer to put them through to the Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning or the
ministry responsible. Do not name a project, a figure or a place that is not on
this page.

## What is confirmed

- **Executive Governor**: His Excellency, Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya. In office
  since **29 May 2019**, serving a second term after re-election in 2023.
- **Deputy Governor**: His Excellency, Manassah Daniel Jatau — not re-confirmed
  for 2026; verify with Government House.
- Gombe State has **11 Local Government Areas** (plus 13 Local Council
  Development Areas created in August 2025, which are not LGAs) and **3
  Senatorial Districts**: Gombe North, Gombe Central and Gombe South.
- Gombe State is in the **North-East** geopolitical zone, and was carved out of
  Bauchi State in **1996**.
- Local council elections were held on **27 June 2026**, after which the
  Governor swore in new council chairmen.
- Two ministries were created in 2026: **Humanitarian Affairs** and **Livestock
  Development**.
- A **40 MW hydropower project** at the Dadin Kowa Dam has been reported as
  commissioned, and the regional water scheme was revived to deliver about **50
  million litres a day** to the Gombe metropolis.
- In **March 2025** the Federal Government took over the newly built **Kumo
  General Hospital** and converted it into a **Federal Medical Centre**.

Those last three are the only delivery items any source read confirms. They are
not a scorecard, and must not be presented as one.

## To whoever fills this in

Compile from Gombe State's own records — the approved budget and capital
releases, the Gombe State Development Plan 2021–2030, Government House releases
at gombestate.gov.ng, and each ministry's project list. One project per line,
each naming the Local Government Area it is in and the date it was flagged off
or commissioned. Nothing goes on this page that a published State source does
not state.
