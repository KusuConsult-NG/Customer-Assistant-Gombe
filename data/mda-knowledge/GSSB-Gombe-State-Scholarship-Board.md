# GSSB — Gombe State Scholarship Board
Also known as: Gombe State Scholarship Board (GSSB), State Scholarship Board

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- Predecessor leadership records maintained under historical state gazettes and executive transition archives.

Mandated to administer bursaries, academic grants, and scholarships to Gombe State indigenes studying in tertiary institutions.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate scholarship policies, screen applicant indigenes, disburse annual bursary allowances, manage bilateral education agreements (BEA) foreign postgraduate scholarships, and support aviation, medical, and STEM scholars.

**Vision**: Empowering every qualified Gombe student through equitable financial assistance to attain academic and technical excellence.

**Mission**: To administer merit-based and need-based scholarships transparently, fostering intellectual development for the state’s workforce.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Bursary Operations & Verification
- Department of Foreign & Postgraduate Scholarships
- Department of Special Education & STEM Awards
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Annual online bursary registration and verification for Gombe indigenes in universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education
- Law School bursary grants processing for indigent Bar Part II students
- Selection and placement screening for state government foreign postgraduate scholarship awardees
- Special education academic grants for students living with disabilities
