Established under the Gombe State Tourism Corporation Law to commercialize and maintain the state’s extraordinary eco-tourism destinations.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Manage and develop state-owned tourist attractions; preserve flora, fauna, and wildlife reserves; promote eco-tourism excursions; and market Gombe State's natural beauty.

**Vision**: To position Gombe State as Nigeria's leading destination for wildlife conservation, nature tourism, and mountain recreation.

**Mission**: To sustainably manage state tourist assets, protect wildlife, and deliver unforgettable visitor experiences.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Wildlife Parks & Game Reserves
- Department of Tourism Destination Marketing
- Department of Hospitality Facilities & Concessions

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Visitor admissions and educational guided tours at Dadin Kowa Dam and Bulok Warm Spring
- Event venue hire at Solomon Lar Amusement Park and tourism resort grounds
- Organizing student nature excursions and wildlife conservation education programmes

## Key State Tourist Destinations Managed
- Dadin Kowa Dam (Akko LGA) — the State's flagship wildlife reserve and Bulok Warm Spring
- Assop Waterfalls (Billiri LGA)
- Kurra Falls & Hydro Resort (Akko LGA)
- Solomon Lar Amusement Park, Gombe
- Shere Hills Mountain Range & Rock Formations
