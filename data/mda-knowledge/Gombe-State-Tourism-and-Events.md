# Gombe State Tourism and Events
Also known as: Tourism in Gombe, events in Gombe State, festivals in Gombe, what to do in Gombe, Gombe State events calendar

## No events calendar is confirmed

Gombe State's events calendar has not been compiled for this service. There is
no lineup here to read out, and no date or venue for any festival, carnival or
exhibition is confirmed.

Tell the caller plainly that we do not have the events calendar, and offer to put
them through to the Ministry responsible for culture and tourism or the Gombe
State Tourism Corporation. Do not name an event, a date or a venue that is not
on this page.

## Tourist destinations the State Government itself lists

- **Dadin Kowa Dam** — on the Gongola River in **Yamaltu/Deba LGA**; also a
  wildlife spot, and the site of a 40 MW hydropower project
- **Tomb of Bubayero**
- **Bima Hill**
- **Kilang Hill**
- **Tula Plateau** and **Tula Cave**
- **Bulok Warm Spring**
- **Bage Hills**
- **Kanawa Forest**

**NOT CONFIRMED for every site above:** which Local Government Area it is in,
how to get there, opening times, entry fees and whether it is currently open to
visitors. Give the name and offer a callback; do not invent directions.
