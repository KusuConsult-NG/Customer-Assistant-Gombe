# HMB — Gombe State Hospitals Management Board
Also known as: Gombe State Hospitals Management Board (HMB), Hospitals Management Board

Administers the network of General Hospitals and Cottage Hospitals throughout the 20 Local Government Areas of Gombe State.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Manage, staff, equip, and supervise all secondary healthcare institutions; ensure uniform clinical standards; coordinate referral links between primary health centres and specialist hospitals; and deploy medical doctors and nursing staff.

**Vision**: An accessible, well-equipped, and responsive secondary healthcare hospital network serving every community in Gombe State.

**Mission**: To provide quality, dignified, and decentralized secondary clinical services across all 20 LGAs through professional healthcare personnel and modern medical technologies.

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Clinical inpatient and outpatient services at the State's general hospitals. Gombe State reports 26 general hospitals; NOT CONFIRMED: no published source names them, and the list previously here belonged to another State. Get the list from the Hospitals Management Board.
- Emergency obstetric care, Cesarean sections, and safe delivery services across rural districts
- Inpatient medical admissions, surgical procedures, and blood bank transfusions
- Medical fitness certificate assessments for employment, school admission, and driver licensing
