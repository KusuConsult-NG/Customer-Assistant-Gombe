# Gombe State — Supplemental Services Guide

## Status of this guide

**Almost everything this guide used to contain was another State's service
data, and has been removed. Where a section says NOT CONFIRMED, tell the caller
we do not have it and offer to put them through.**

What stood here was a detailed operational guide: tax rates and bands, a
scholarship schedule with naira amounts, the twenty LGA chairmen by name, a full
set of emergency hotline numbers, a ministry telephone directory, metro bus
routes and fares, land registry fees and processing times, ICT programme intake
details, and a hospital-by-hospital accredited-facility list for every LGA. All
of it belonged to **a different State's** agencies, and the rebrand had replaced
only the place names, leaving the numbers, the fees, the routes, the hospitals
and the officials' names intact beneath Gombe headings.

This is the guide a citizen acts on: they pay the fee, they travel to the
hospital, they ring the number, they board the bus. Nothing in it can be
approximated, so each section now states what is actually known.

---

## 1. Revenue and tax — Gombe State Board of Internal Revenue

- The State revenue authority is the **Gombe State Board of Internal Revenue**.
- Typical functions: Personal Income Tax (PAYE), direct assessment for informal
  traders and sole proprietors, tax clearance certificates, land use charge,
  stamp duties, and motor vehicle licensing and road taxes.
- **Executive Chairman, office address, telephone, every rate, band, deadline,
  penalty and portal: NOT CONFIRMED.**

Never quote a tax rate, a band or a penalty. Refer the caller to the Board.

## 3. Local Government Chairmen

- **NOT CONFIRMED for any of the 11 LGAs.** The list that stood here named the
  chairmen of another State's councils. Take the current list from the Ministry
  for Local Government and Chieftaincy Affairs, or from the Local Government
  Service Commission.

---

## 4. Emergency and crisis helplines

- **112** is the national emergency number, and the only number this service
  states on its own.
- Every other number is set per agency at **Settings → Emergency contacts** and
  is read out verbatim from there. **No helpline number is written on this
  page**, because the ones that were here belonged to another State's agencies,
  including a security outfit that does not operate in Gombe at all.

On an emergency: give 112 and the agency's own configured contacts, say an
officer has been alerted, and never say that responders have been dispatched.

---

## 8. ICT and digital services

- **Programmes, intakes, eligibility and how to apply: NOT CONFIRMED.**

---

## 9. GoHealth accredited hospitals

- Confirmed by name, and only these five:
  - **Gombe State Specialist Hospital** (State) and **Federal Teaching
    Hospital, Gombe** (federal, ~500 beds) — both in **Gombe LGA**. They are
    different institutions with similar names; establish which one a caller
    means.
  - **Federal Medical Centre, Kumo** (federal since March 2025) — **Akko LGA**.
  - **General Hospital, Kaltungo** — **Kaltungo LGA**, the Gombe South zonal
    hospital.
  - **General Hospital, Bajoga** — **Funakaye LGA**, the Gombe North zonal
    hospital.
- **Seven of the 11 LGAs have no confirmed facility**, and no published source
  names the State's primary health centres. There is **no LGA-by-LGA accredited
  list**.
- For any other LGA, say we do not have the accredited list for that LGA and
  direct the caller to their **nearest General Hospital or Primary Health
  Centre**, or to GoHealth.

Load the real directory with `npm run catalogue:seed` once GoHealth supplies it;
`lgasWithoutFacilities()` reports which of the 11 LGAs are still uncovered.
