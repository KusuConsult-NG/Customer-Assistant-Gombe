The premier commercial investment vehicle of the Gombe State Government, managing state equities and commercial properties.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Manage the state government's equity portfolios in quoted and unquoted companies; develop and manage commercial real estate; promote joint venture industrial investments; and unlock wealth from state assets.

**Vision**: A premier sovereign investment corporation driving sustainable industrialization and wealth for Gombe State.

**Mission**: To maximize return on state capital assets, attract global private investments, and develop strategic commercial enterprises.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Portfolio Investments & Capital Markets
- Department of Real Estate Development & Property Management
- Department of Business Development & Joint Ventures

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Commercial office space and shopping plaza leasing in GIPC commercial properties
- Joint-venture investment partnerships in agro-processing, tourism, and real estate
- Verification of state shareholdings and investment asset disclosures
