# APC Governorship Candidate Manifesto — Dr. Jamil Isyaku Gwamna (The IMPACTS Manifesto, 2027–2031)
Also known as: Gombe APC governorship manifesto, Dr. Jamilu Isyaku Gwamna manifesto, Sardaunan Gombe manifesto, IMPACTS Agenda, A Greater Gombe Together, 2027 Gombe APC governorship candidate promises

## Overview & Candidate Details
- **Candidate:** Dr. Jamil Isyaku Gwamna, PhD (also spelled Jamilu Isiyaku Gwamna; traditional title: **Sardaunan Gombe**)
- **Party & Office:** All Progressives Congress (APC) Governorship Candidate for Gombe State, 2027 General Election (won the Gombe APC governorship primary in May 2026)
- **Manifesto Title:** *The IMPACTS Manifesto (2027–2031): A Greater Gombe Together — My Covenant with the People of Gombe State*
- **Date Unveiled:** Monday, November 16, 2026, at the Conference Hall, APC Square, Gombe
- **Official Source:** [www.jig-impacts.com](https://www.jig-impacts.com/) ([English PDF](https://www.jig-impacts.com/IMPACTS-Manifesto-English.pdf)); corroborating coverage in Leadership Newspapers (Nov 16, 2026), The Nigeria Standard (Nov 17, 2026), and PeriscopeNGA (Nov 18, 2026)
- **Administrative Scope:** Uses "LGA" for Gombe State's 11 Local Government Areas and "LCDA" for Local Council Development Areas established by Gombe State House of Assembly law. Commitments assigned to an LCDA are to be planned, budgeted, implemented, and reported at the LCDA level.

## Vision (2027–2031)
Transform Gombe State into:
1. Northern Nigeria's leading agro-industrial and logistics hub
2. Northern Nigeria's premier conference, tourism, hospitality, and cultural destination
3. A youth-powered digital innovation centre
4. A state where every child learns in a safe school
5. A state where water, sanitation, drainage, and erosion control protect dignity and life
6. A state where jobs, enterprise, tourism, hospitality, and inclusive growth reach every household

Core message of continuity: *"I am not here to reinvent the wheel. I am here to ensure that the wheel keeps turning, and that it carries all of us forward,"* building on the foundation of incumbent Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya, CON (Dan Majen Gombe).

## Headline Four-Year Targets (by 2031)
- **100,000 net new jobs** across all sectors (measured without double counting, supported by sector-level reporting), including:
  - **10,000 direct manufacturing jobs**
  - **50,000 indirect manufacturing and value-chain jobs**
  - **20,000 direct and indirect livestock value-chain jobs**
  - **20,000 direct and indirect tourism, hospitality, transport, arts, entertainment, food services, and cultural jobs**
  - **At least 50,000 direct and indirect jobs** across commerce, trade, tourism, hospitality, and related services
- **1,000 km** of additional statewide roads (Network 11/100 2.0)
- **10,000 affordable housing units or serviced plots**
- **100 MW solar power plant** through private-sector investment
- **3 serviced industrial or agro-processing clusters** operational by 2030 (including Phase 2 of the Muhammadu Buhari Industrial Park)
- **50,000 SMEs** supported (at least 40% women-led and 35% youth-led)
- **110 functional agricultural clusters** across all 11 LGAs and **100 functional dairy cooperatives**
- **400,000 out-of-school children** returned to classrooms (100,000 per year over four years)
- **90% digital literacy** across public primary and secondary schools by 2031
- **150,000 youths** trained in digital skills (G-ODA) and **100,000 verified digital income opportunities** supported
- **2 fully functioning, equipped primary healthcare (PHC) facilities per ward** (24-hour service) and **1 million Go-Health beneficiaries**
- **4 million trees** planted over four years (Gombe Goes Green 3G) and **1,000 community-based environmental rangers** trained
- **Quarterly public scorecards** tracking delivery

## The Seven Pillars — IMPACTS

### Pillar 1 (I): Infrastructure & Essential Services
*Guiding principle: Complete ongoing projects, maintain existing assets, expand essential services, and build the infrastructure Gombe needs.*

- **1.1 Complete Ongoing Projects:** Complete every road, bridge, solar streetlight, and other infrastructure project initiated by Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya's administration according to schedule.
- **1.2 Rural Connectivity (Network 11/100 2.0):** Build **1,000 km** of additional statewide roads allocated to LGAs/LCDAs by need and economic impact, giving every farming community a motorable route to the nearest market; partner with the Federal Government and Development Finance Institutions.
- **1.3 Housing & Urban Renewal:** Deliver or enable at least **10,000 affordable housing units or serviced plots by 2031** via serviced land, transparent allocation, mortgage support, rent-to-own, cooperative housing, climate-resilient standards, and PPPs (priority windows for low/middle-income families, civil servants, women-headed households, and PWDs). Enforce the Gombe Capital Development Zone Executive Order through GOSUPDA and GOGIS. Build suburb township roads, complete the **Gombe Northern Bypass**, reconstruct and expand the **Gombe Southern Bypass**, and establish public parks and recreational spaces in every LCDA.
- **1.4 Maintenance Agency:** Create a dedicated **Infrastructure Maintenance & Facility Management Agency** for roads, streetlights, and public buildings.
- **1.5 Water Supply & Sanitation:** Complete the **Gombe Regional Water Project**, overhaul the **Gombe Water Corporation**, and construct or refurbish **50 boreholes in each LCDA** over four years (with an annual published LCDA-level borehole plan and functionality reporting). Build additional waste disposal facilities in each of the **114 wards**, employ sanitation marshals, and partner with the private sector on an integrated waste recycling facility in Gombe metropolis.
- **1.6 Water & Sanitation Targets (by 2031):** Increase reliable water-supply coverage in priority towns by at least **30%** from the 2027 baseline; achieve at least **12 hours of daily supply** in serviced zones; reduce non-revenue water by at least **25%**; map all major gully-erosion and flood hotspots; stabilise/rehabilitate at least **10 high-risk gully sites** and **20 priority drainage corridors**; cut recurring flood/erosion damage to priority public assets by at least **50%**.
- **1.7 Transport & Energy:** Partner with investors for a **100 MW solar power plant** for off-grid industrial and domestic energy sufficiency. Introduce **electric buses** inside Gombe metropolis and **CNG-powered buses** connecting major towns, with EV charging and CNG refuelling stations in Gombe metropolis and every LCDA headquarters. Install solar streetlights and upgrade roads/lighting/signage to Dadin Kowa Dam, Tula Highlands, Tangale Hills, Kilang Hills, and Cham Valley.
- **1.8 Regional Infrastructure (Federal & Neighbouring-State Advocacy):** Dualisation of Abuja–Akwanga–Jos–Bauchi–Gombe road; Gombe–Biu Super Highway; Gombe–Bajoga–Nafada road; Gombe–Dukku–Darazo road; regional railway, electricity transmission, and broadband projects.

### Pillar 2 (M): Manufacturing & Industrialisation
*Guiding principle: Move from exporting raw materials to exporting processed or finished goods ("From Farm to Factories, Gombe to the World").*

- **2.1 Agro-Mechanisation & Agro-Processing:** Complete and operationalise the **Special Agro-Livestock Development Zone**; leverage Gombe State's **SAPZ** status; support and expand Gombe Agro-MSME clusters (rice millers, oil extractors, etc.) with clustered energy and modern equipment.
- **2.2 Industrial Revitalisation:** Complete and operationalise **Phase 2 of the Muhammadu Buhari Industrial Park** as one of at least **three serviced industrial or agro-processing clusters operational by 2030** (with reliable power, internal roads, water, drainage, security, waste management, and business support). Partner on the **100 MW solar power plant**, support manufacturers/MSMEs with loans, grants, and capacity building, and engage the Federal Government to sustain **oil exploration at Kolmani**.
- **2.3 One LCDA, One Product:** Each LCDA identifies and develops one flagship product based on comparative advantage, local skills, raw materials, market demand, and sustainability. Within Year 1, publish an LCDA product map and 4-year delivery plan; organise transparent producer cooperatives; provide shared processing, power, water, storage, testing, packaging, logistics, and digital market access; create a dedicated blended financing window (grants, credit, equipment leasing, private capital); support certification, branding, traceability, and export linkages; guarantee participation for women, youth, and PWDs; publish an annual LCDA scorecard.
- **2.4 Manufacturing Employment Targets (by 2031):** Create **10,000 direct manufacturing jobs** and **50,000 indirect manufacturing and value-chain jobs** (60,000 total across Renewed Hope Agro-Industrial Hub, Phase 2 of Muhammadu Buhari Industrial Park, the 3 industrial clusters, and One LCDA, One Product), tracked via quarterly scorecards without double counting.

### Pillar 3 (P): People & Human Capital Development
*Guiding principle: Every child educated, every mother safe, every young person skilled, and every woman empowered.*

- **3.1 & 3.2 Education Commitments & Targets (by 2031):**
  - Recruit **1,000 new teachers** and train/mentor/certify **10,000 existing teachers** (Years 1–2, reaching ≥80% of the verified 2027 teacher workforce).
  - Return **400,000 out-of-school children** to classrooms (**100,000 per year**, Years 1–4, distinguishing newly enrolled from retained).
  - Build **1 additional Mega Secondary School (Mega College) per senatorial zone** (Years 2–3) and revive technical and vocational colleges in all three senatorial zones (Years 2–3).
  - Achieve **90% digital literacy** in primary and secondary schools by 2031.
  - Upgrade at least **60% of public basic schools**; improve literacy and numeracy outcomes by at least **35%** from the 2027 baseline.
  - Provide targeted tertiary scholarships for at least **10,000 students from poor households**, support at least **3,000 bonded students** in critical professions, provide **school feeding for at least 250,000 children** from poor households, and reach at least **200,000 vulnerable households** with linked social protection.
- **3.3 Healthcare Commitments:**
  - Maintain and equip at least **2 fully functioning PHC facilities per ward** (24-hour service; Years 1–2).
  - Upgrade or construct **3 General Hospitals** — one in each senatorial zone (Year 2).
  - **Gombe Mothers' Health Initiative:** Free antenatal, delivery, and postnatal care (Year 1).
  - Recruit **500 health workers** (doctors, nurses, midwives, pharmacists, community health workers) for rural areas (Years 1–2).
  - Expand **Go-Health** contributory health insurance to **1 million beneficiaries** (Years 1–4).
- **3.4 Youth Empowerment:** **Gombe Youth Enterprise Fund** (single-digit low-interest loans with flexible repayment for ages 18–35); **Vocational Skills Development Centres** (one per senatorial zone: ICT, fashion, catering, hairdressing, welding, tailoring, etc.); train and support **5,000 youths annually** (separate from G-ODA); **40% youth inclusion in governance**.
- **3.5 Student Affairs:** **Free NECO, WAEC, and JAMB** for all final-year secondary school students; increase student scholarship bursaries; review and reduce tuition and registration fees for Gombe State indigenes in state-owned tertiary institutions; merit- and needs-based foreign scholarships; **3,000 bonded sponsorships** in health, education, engineering, agriculture, and technology with post-graduation service in Gombe State.
- **3.6 Women, Children & Family:** **Women Economic Empowerment Programme** (training, grants/loans, market access for **10,000 direct women beneficiaries** over four years); reserve market stalls for women in newly rehabilitated markets; **35% government appointments reserved for women**; maternal and child nutrition (including RUTF against stunting/wasting); build or upgrade **one girl-only boarding secondary school in each LCDA**; family welfare and social security package.
- **3.7 Sports, Recreation & Creative Enterprise:** Sports and recreation centres in each LCDA; establish **Gombe Sports Management Company**; overhaul **Gombe United FC** and state-owned clubs; promote sporting events, conferences, cultural exhibitions, and creative industries.
- **3.8 Civil Service Reforms:** Transition to e-governance and digital workflows; training and capacity building; timely promotions and prompt payment of salaries, pensions, and gratuities.
- **3.9 People with Special Needs (PWDs):** Strengthen the **Gombe State Disability Commission**; skills and enterprise support; **free health coverage for PWDs through Go-Health**; build or upgrade **special needs schools in each senatorial zone**.
- **3.10 Public Service Targets (by 2031):** Digitise at least **20 high-demand government services**; resolve at least **70% of registered citizen complaints** within approved service timelines; digitise personnel records and train at least **80% of civil servants**; verify all outstanding retirement claims in Year 1, begin phased settlement in Year 2, and reduce verified outstanding claims by at least **80% by 2031**; ensure at least **10% inclusion of PWDs or vulnerable groups** across skills, SME, housing, scholarship, and social-protection programmes.

### Pillar 4 (A): Agriculture & Rural Transformation
*Guiding principle: Expand mechanisation, increase yields, reduce post-harvest losses, and strengthen rural economies.*

- **4.1 Irrigation & Dry-Season Farming:** Expand irrigation at **Dadin Kowa, Balanga, and Cham dams** (including along Dadin Kowa Dam in **Yamaltu/Deba, Funakaye, Nafada, and Dukku** LGAs); construct earth dams; subsidise water pumps, seeds, and fertilisers; deliver mechanisation and extension via Go-Tech Innovation Hub and GSU College of Agriculture.
- **4.2 Agricultural Production Targets (by 2031):** At least **110 functional agricultural cluster groups across all 11 LGAs**; increase average yields of priority crops (maize, millet, beans, sorghum, rice) by at least **25%**; expand functional irrigated farmland by at least **35%**; reduce post-harvest losses by **25%**; support at least **8 major or medium-scale agro-processing facilities**; raise participating farmers' incomes by **25%** with at least **40% women and youth participation**.
- **4.3 Post-Harvest Infrastructure:** Cold storage units in all major farming communities (Years 1–2); drying slabs for tomatoes/peppers in **100 communities** (Year 1); rural feeder roads prioritised by harvest volume (Years 1–3); real-time market information systems (Years 1–4); fintech partnerships with rural microfinance banks for credit, insurance, and inputs (Years 1–4).
- **4.4 Mechanisation & Extension:** Recruit and deploy **300 extension officers**; establish **demonstration farms in each LCDA**; mobile phone advisory services (weather, pest alerts, market prices); **mechanisation hubs in each LCDA** with tractors, drones, and leaseable equipment.
- **4.5 & 4.6 Livestock Modernisation & Targets (by 2031):** Establish a **Ministry of Livestock Development** (cattle fattening, dairy processing, leather); complete the **Special Agro-Livestock Development Zone**; canalise **Wawa-Zange Grazing Reserve** into designated ranching zones with water points and vet clinics. Targets: upgrade at least **6 priority livestock service zones**; support **100 functional dairy cooperatives**; expand animal-health/veterinary coverage by **30%**; grow formal milk collection/dairy participation by **25%**; modernise **4 abattoir/meat-processing/livestock-market facilities**; support **20,000 direct and indirect livestock value-chain jobs**.
- **4.7 Agritourism & Rural Enterprise:** Farm tourism, livestock exhibitions, agricultural fairs, food festivals, and community-owned tourism around dams, forests, grazing reserves, and rural heritage sites.
- **4.8 Farmer-Herder Conflict Prevention:** Establish **Farmer-Herder Peace Councils** in every affected LCDA; restore and protect grazing routes, grazing reserves, and forest reserves; reject sweeping criminalisation of herders while enforcing clear consequences and compensation for farmland trespass and destruction.

### Pillar 5 (C): Commerce, Trade & Tourism
*Guiding principle: Empower small businesses, expand trade, and establish Gombe as Northern Nigeria's leading commercial centre after Kano.*

- **5.1 Business Support:** **Trader Support Fund** (with Bank of Industry and SMEDAN for revolving loans to petty traders, market women, and small business owners); private-sector commercial layouts for shopping malls in Gombe metropolis and major towns.
- **5.2 Market Rehabilitation:** Modernise **Gombe Main Market, Gombe Old Market, Kasuwar Tike**, and **one model market in each senatorial zone** (clean water, toilets, waste disposal, security lighting, fire safety, solar-powered cold storage).
- **5.3 One-Stop Business Registration Desk & SME Targets (by 2031):** Strengthen GOSIPA and GIRS for **48-hour business registration** (one form, one office, one fee schedule) plus free tax/business/credit advisory. Support at least **50,000 SMEs** (**≥40% women-led, ≥35% youth-led**) with annual public reporting.
- **5.4 & 5.5 Tourism, Culture & Hospitality:** Comprehensive **Gombe State Tourism and Hospitality Master Plan**; position **Gombe International Conference Centre (GICC)** as the North-East's premier conference destination; facilitate a **5-Star Hotel, Convention Village, and Recreation Resort** in the GICC–Airport corridor; upgrade **Gombe International Hotel, Jewel Hotel Gombe, and Kaltungo Jewel Hotel** via PPPs; develop **Dadin Kowa Dam & Reservoir**, **Tula Highlands & Plateau**, **Tangale Hills, Kilang Hill, Kalam Hill, Bima Hills, and Cham Valley**; preserve cultural heritage of Gombe Emirate, Tangale, Tula, Waja, Tera, Bolewa, Fulani, and other communities; institutionalise an **Annual Tourism and Cultural Festival Calendar**; launch **Visit Gombe Initiative** ("The Jewel in the Savannah").
  - **Tourism Targets (by 2031):** At least **250,000 domestic and international visitors annually**; at least **1 flagship tourism project per senatorial zone**; **20,000 direct and indirect tourism/hospitality/culture jobs**; Top 5 Northern Nigeria conference, leisure, and eco-tourism destination.
- **5.6 Overall Job Creation Target:** **100,000 net new jobs** across all sectors over four years, including at least **50,000 direct and indirect jobs** through commerce, trade, tourism, hospitality, transport, arts, culture, entertainment, and related services.

### Pillar 6 (T): Technology & Digital Inclusion
*Guiding principle: A Gombe that is not digital will be left behind.*

- **6.1 Connectivity & Broadband:** Extend broadband internet to all LCDAs via PPPs; free public Wi-Fi in selected parks, markets, motor parks, hospitals, and public spaces; encourage local digital payments.
- **6.2 Gombe Digital Opportunities and Advancement (G-ODA):** Central programme office with **one mini-hub in each senatorial district**; train youths in coding/software development, graphic design, digital marketing, data analysis, e-commerce, and online business management; connect trainees to Nigerian and international remote work.
  - **Digital Targets (by 2031):** Train at least **150,000 youths**; support at least **100,000 verified digital income opportunities** (only verified paid jobs and sustained self-employment count toward the 100,000 net-new-jobs headline; other income activities reported separately in quarterly scorecards).
- **6.3 E-Governance & Citizen Participation:** Single e-governance portal and mobile channels for land records, business permits, and tax services; public project tracker (budgets, procurement, contracts, locations, completion status, expenditure, verified photos); multi-channel citizen complaints (portal, SMS, USSD, call centres, help desks) with reference numbers; online consultations, participatory budgeting, and assisted access at LCDA offices and hubs (including accessible formats for PWDs).
- **6.4 Digital Tourism Promotion:** **Visit Gombe Digital Platform** for attractions, hotels, events, transport, online booking, and digital payments.
- **6.5 Technology in Education:** **Gombe Digital Literacy Programme (Primary 1 to JSS 3)**; digital literacy centres in public secondary schools; teacher training and child online-safety standards; achieve **90% digital literacy** across public primary and secondary schools by 2031.

### Pillar 7 (S): Security & Environment
*Guiding principle: No security without environmental healing. No environmental sustainability without security.*

- **7.1 Security Measures:** Support constitutionally permitted reforms for state-level and community policing alongside traditional institutions, recognised community-security structures, and federal agencies; **Farmer-Herder Peace Councils in every LCDA**; border security coordination with neighbouring states; youth skills/jobs as a security strategy; strengthened security around tourism and conference sites.
- **7.2 Environmental Rangers:** Train **1,000 community-based environmental rangers and forest guards** (supported by security agencies) to protect forests, watersheds, and grazing reserves from illegal logging, bush burning, and encroachment.
- **7.3 Security–Environment Nexus:** Address deforestation, land degradation, gully erosion, and drought/crop failure as interconnected drivers of rural insecurity and livelihood loss.
- **7.4 Reforestation & Afforestation (3G):** Re-energise **Gombe Goes Green (3G)** to plant **4 million trees in four years** (independently monitored), with community/household/school tree ownership, northern frontline shelterbelts against desertification, and green bonds/carbon financing.
- **7.5 Combating Desertification & Land Degradation:** Sustainable land management training (crop rotation, mulching, contour ploughing); revitalise grazing reserves with water points and fodder banks; terracing, retention walls, and grass planting.
- **7.6 Erosion & Flood Control:** Complete ongoing gully erosion projects and start new ones; local weather stations and early-warning systems with NiMet; enforce riverbank and wetland buffer zones.
- **7.7 Waste Management & Pollution Control:** Material recovery and recycling facilities (plastic, paper, glass, metals); regulated scrap recovery with registration and designated collection points; monthly community sanitation rewarding cleanest LCDAs, wards, and markets.
- **7.8 Climate Adaptation & Resilient Livelihoods:** Demonstration farm in every LCDA by 2031 with 300 extension officers (Ministry of Agriculture); 4-million-tree and land restoration drive (Environment & Livestock Development); map all major flood/erosion hotspots, stabilise **≥10 high-risk gully sites**, rehabilitate **20 priority drainage corridors**, and cut recurring asset damage by **≥50% by 2031** (Environment); pilot affordable **index-based agricultural insurance** in high-risk communities (Ministry of Agriculture).

## Governing Philosophy & Accountability Mechanisms
- **Four Governing Principles:**
  1. **Accountability** — annual and quarterly public scorecards showing what was promised, delivered, and remains outstanding.
  2. **Continuity** — protect, complete, and expand Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya's legacy and working programmes.
  3. **Fiscal Discipline** — maintain prudent financial management and ensure every naira creates lasting value.
  4. **Inclusion** — no individual or community left behind regardless of geography, gender, ethnicity, religion, or social status.
- **Five Built-In Accountability Tools:**
  1. **Project Status Dashboard** (location, budget, contractor, completion percentage, photos)
  2. **Budget Performance Report** (budgeted vs. spent vs. achieved)
  3. **IMPACTS Progress Tracker** (every commitment marked Not Started, Ongoing, Completed, or Delayed)
  4. **Audited Financial Statements** (published within statutory timelines)
  5. **Citizen Feedback Report** (complaints received, investigated, and resolved)
