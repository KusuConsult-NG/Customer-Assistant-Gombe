# Gombe State Government — Citizen Service and State Guide

## How to use this guide

Everything on this page is either confirmed by a published source read on
2026-11-21, or is marked **NOT CONFIRMED**. Where something is marked not
confirmed, say so and offer to put the caller through to the agency. Never fill
a gap on this page with a name, a figure, a title, an address or a date that is
not written here.

## 2. The 11 Local Government Areas of Gombe State

Gombe State has **11 Local Government Areas** and **3 Senatorial Districts**
(Gombe North, Gombe Central, Gombe South). The 11, alphabetically:

Akko · Balanga · Billiri · Dukku · Funakaye · Gombe · Kaltungo · Kwami ·
Nafada · Shongom · Yamaltu/Deba

The State capital is **Gombe**, in Gombe LGA. "Billiri" is also written
"Biliri"; "Yamaltu/Deba" is commonly shortened to "Deba", its headquarters
town.

**The 13 Local Council Development Areas** created by the bill the Governor
signed in **August 2025** are **not** Local Government Areas. An LCDA is a
State administrative unit; the 11 LGAs are the ones recognised in the
Constitution. The two are often reported together as "24 administrative
units" — do not register a citizen against an LCDA.

**Full Senatorial District composition: NOT CONFIRMED.** What is confirmed is
that the State's three zonal general hospitals sit one per district: **Kumo
(Gombe Central)**, **Kaltungo (Gombe South)** and **Bajoga, in Funakaye
(Gombe North)**.

**LGA headquarters, principal towns and institutions per LGA: NOT CONFIRMED**
beyond the headquarters towns named above and in the service directory.

## 4. GoHealth — the State health insurance scheme

The **Gombe State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency** runs the State's
contributory healthcare scheme, known as **GoHealth** (also written GO-HEALTH
or GOHEALTH). The enabling law was passed after the national health insurance
scheme was decentralised, which also opened the State's access to the federal
**Basic Healthcare Provision Fund**.

- **Chief executive, headquarters address and telephone**: NOT CONFIRMED.
- **Contribution, public-sector workers**: the worker pays **3.5% of gross
  salary** and the State Government **matches** it.
- **Coverage**: a 2025 paper by agency and State health officials reports the
  scheme covering **over 380,000 residents**; an earlier government statement
  put enrolment at over 140,000.
- **Equity programmes**: the agency has enrolled **1,000 non-formal school
  children** and launched enrolment of **orphans** under an equity programme,
  and has moved to enrol local government workers and tertiary students.

**Premium rates for other sectors, the benefits package, and the accredited
facility list: NOT CONFIRMED.** Quoting a premium a citizen then cannot pay, or
sending them to a facility that is not accredited, is worse than telling them
to ask. Get the current rates, benefits and accredited-facility list from the
agency and load them with `npm run catalogue:seed`.

### Facilities confirmed by name

- **Gombe State Specialist Hospital**, Gombe LGA — the State referral hospital,
  rebuilt after 2019; the State reports close to 20 specialist consultants.
- **Federal Teaching Hospital, Gombe**, Gombe LGA — **federal**, around 500
  beds, upgraded from a federal medical centre. It is a different institution
  from the State Specialist Hospital, so establish which one a caller means.
- **Federal Medical Centre, Kumo**, Akko LGA — **federal** since March 2025,
  when the Federal Government took over the newly built Kumo General Hospital.
  Reported as also serving Federal University Kashere and Lincoln University,
  Kumo.
- **General Hospital, Kaltungo**, Kaltungo LGA — the Gombe South zonal hospital.
- **General Hospital, Bajoga**, Funakaye LGA — the Gombe North zonal hospital.

Seven of the 11 LGAs have no confirmed facility. For those, say we do not have
the list for that area and direct the caller to their nearest General Hospital
or Primary Health Centre.

## 6. Water supply

The **Dadin Kowa Dam**, on the **Gongola River** in **Yamaltu/Deba LGA**, is
the State's principal water source. The regional water scheme was revived to
deliver about **50 million litres a day** to the Gombe metropolis and its
surroundings; one report puts the potential at 50 to 70 million litres with the
expansion under way. The dam also serves **irrigation** and carries a **40 MW
hydropower** project reported as commissioned.

Sources disagree on the dam's distance from Gombe town (35 km and 41.6 km both
appear) and on its completion year (1984, 1986 and 1988 all appear), so state
neither. The body responsible for urban distribution and its enabling law are
**NOT CONFIRMED**.

## 9. Emergency Numbers and Public Helplines

- **112** is the national emergency number. It is the only number this service
  states without the agency having supplied it.
- The State's own emergency and helpline numbers are set per agency at
  Settings → Emergency contacts, and are read out verbatim from there. **None
  is hardcoded on this page.**

If a caller reports an emergency, give **112** and whatever the agency has put
in its emergency contacts, say an officer has been alerted, and never say
responders have been dispatched.

## 11. One thing worth knowing about this State's records

Gombe State was **carved out of Bauchi State in 1996**. The two share pre-1996
history and are frequently covered together in the press, so a Bauchi fact can
read as plausibly Gombe's and is not: Yankari Game Reserve, the Gubi Dam,
Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University and BASHCMA are all **Bauchi's**, not Gombe's.
If a source does not say Gombe, it is not a Gombe source.
