Also known as: Ministry of Information

The chief communications organ of the Gombe State Government, managing the government’s public enlightenment, press relations, and state broadcasting media.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate information policies, disseminate government programs and accomplishments, manage press relations, accredit journalists, combat misinformation, and oversee state public media broadcasting corporations.

**Vision**: A transparent, well-informed, and actively engaged Gombe citizenry with open channels of communication between the government and the people.

**Mission**: To project Gombe State positively, ensure prompt and truthful information dissemination, and foster citizen participation in governance through modern multi-channel media.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Public Enlightenment & Information Dissemination
- Department of Media Relations & Press Affairs
- Department of Publications & Documentation
- Department of Audio-Visual & Photographic Services
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- State government press releases, daily news bulletins, and official gazette publications
- Accreditation and facilitation for local, national, and international media correspondents
- Citizen grievance and inquiry resolution through official government helplines and platforms
- Town hall meeting scheduling and public feedback collection on state policies

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
- Gombe Radio Television Corporation (GRTVC Gombe)
- Gombe Printing Press
